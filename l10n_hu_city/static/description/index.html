Populate your Odoo with the official Hungarian city and postal code database

…and automatically link your existing partners to the right cities.

Hungarian addresses are notoriously tricky: postal codes, city names, and counties don't always follow obvious patterns. The city of Budapest alone has dozens of districts with their own postal codes, and smaller settlements can share the same code. Manually entering city data is slow, inconsistent, and prone to error.

Hungarian City Database – Official Postal List solves this by shipping the official Magyar Posta (Hungarian Post) city list directly into your Odoo system. Every city is pre-loaded with its postal code and correct county (state). Better yet, a smart post-install process automatically links your existing Hungarian partners to the right city records – so your data is immediately consistent and correct.

Key Features

📮 Official Hungarian City List Included
Ships with the complete, official city database from Magyar Posta – the authoritative source for Hungarian postal data.

Every city record includes:

  • Postal code (zipcode)
  • City name
  • County (state) – correctly linked to the right Hungarian county

Covers all 19 counties plus Budapest, with thousands of settlements – from small villages to the largest cities.

🏛️ Complete Hungarian County (State) Definitions
All Hungarian counties are pre-defined with their standard codes (e.g., BU for Budapest, BK for Bács‑Kiskun, PE for Pest).
  • Cities are automatically linked to the correct county – no manual assignment.
  • State data is ready to use in addresses, reports, and shipping documents.
🔄 Automatic Migration of Existing Partners
When you install the module, a post-install process runs automatically. It scans all your existing Hungarian partners and matches their current zip and city text fields to the new city records.

Matching is intelligent:

  1. First tries postal code (the most reliable match)
  2. If multiple cities share a code, it narrows the match using the city name
  3. Falls back to matching by city name if the postal code is missing

When a match is found, the partner is automatically updated with:

  • The correct city record linked
  • The standardized city name
  • The correct county / state
📊 Clear Migration Logging
The migration process logs its progress, so you know exactly how many partners were processed.
  • Matched and unresolved partners are counted and reported.
  • Partners that couldn't be resolved are logged with their details.
  • Review and fix unresolved records manually if needed.
🛡️ Safe & Non-Destructive
  • Only partners with no city already linked are touched – your verified data is never overwritten.
  • The migration is idempotent – running it again won't cause issues.
  • If the base Hungarian country record is missing, the process safely skips and logs a warning.
⚡ Efficient Batch Processing
  • All Hungarian city records are loaded once and indexed by postal code and city name.
  • Database queries are minimized – the migration runs quickly, even with thousands of partners.
  • Perfect for large installations with many historical records.

Tutorial

This section explains the most important thing to know before using the module.

Note: You have to choose the country first — only after that can you see the drop-down menu.

Quick steps

  1. Install the module from the Apps menu.
  2. Open a partner (Contact) form.
  3. Select Hungary as the country first.
  4. The City field then becomes a searchable drop-down, populated from the official Magyar Posta list.
  5. Pick the city – the postal code and county fill in automatically.
